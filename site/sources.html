Game/branch/server scope

The 42 catalog entries are a retained working set, not a verified complete ORIGIN mutation inventory: 41 current-reference matches plus the historically removed Traumatic Thrombosis. Experimental Hordetest-only mutations and previously removed Intraspecific Aggression are intentionally not selectable. ORIGIN's server website documents custom features, but does not establish each mutation or combat parameter.

Evidence status: official = published game update; server-published = the server's own statement; community-reported = third-party observation; disputed = source differences or unverified applicability; unverified = evidence unavailable; model assumption = chosen by this site's authors.

Important unresolved questions

  • Whether ORIGIN permits omnivores to pick every mutation labelled herbivore elsewhere; source descriptions differ, so conservative restrictions remain.
  • Nesting inherits parental choices, not necessarily the whole Entomb lineage. Exact parent-to-child slots and exceptions need game or server confirmation.
  • Exact unlock counts and durations are patch-sensitive and not verified for ORIGIN; the in-game mutation menu is the operational reference.
  • Current ORIGIN version, special mutation overrides, and experimental branches are not proven by the listed public website.
  • Prime growth code peaks at 87%, while a community elder guide says 87.5%. The model's non-Prime curve increases to 100%, while that guide describes late frail decline. Both differences remain unresolved; the calculator curves are unchanged.
  • Species masses, hit points, damage, growth-dependent weights, and multiplicative stacking are not established as an accurate live PvP simulation.

Calculation walkthrough

The software combines sourced mutation *effect descriptions* with site-authored assumptions. A cited 5% Hemomania effect does not validate the overall 1v1 ratio or chance of victory. The older growth ladder is a separate heuristic with different multipliers and thresholds.
