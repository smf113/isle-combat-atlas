Important unresolved questions

  • Gallimimus, Beipiaosaurus and Oviraptor: Barometric Sensitivity, Hypervigilance, Photosynthetic Regeneration, Tactile Endurance, Truculency and Xerocole Adaptation are disputed between Isla Sonora and Evrima Quick Guide. All 18 assignments remain unverified on Official 0.22.159 and separately on ORIGIN Main/Voice. Capture build-stamped menus for each sex, growth/Prime state, slots, unlocks and inherited levels. Oviraptor's entire mutation pool and combat stats remain unverified. Its empty species catalog reflects missing eligibility evidence, not an absence of mutations.
  • Public 0.22.157 preserves parental mutation levels in hatchlings and permits one stored Entomb per species. Public 0.22.159 prevents the same mutation being inherited twice across different parental slots. Exact allocation, level conflict resolution and server overrides need in-game evidence; this planner does not simulate hatchling inheritance.
  • Unlock conditions, including counts and durations, are not independently verified for the selected profile. Historical recipes remain in the mutation evidence below; the player catalog shows only “Unlock required” rather than implying that part of an old recipe is a current unlock condition. The in-game menu is the operational reference. Current slot, diet and sex restrictions are conservative catalog rules, not current independently verified menus. The player catalog omits disputed assignments, removed entries and unsupported numerical effects; its build eligibility model is unchanged.
  • Current ORIGIN version, special mutation overrides, and experimental branches are not proven by the listed public website. Selecting an ORIGIN profile does not make community mutation or combat estimates server-verified.
  • Prime growth code peaks at 87%, while a community elder guide says 87.5%. The model's non-Prime curve increases to 100%, while that guide describes late frail decline. Both differences remain unresolved; the calculator curves are unchanged.
  • Species masses, hit points, damage, growth-dependent weights, and multiplicative stacking are not established as an accurate live PvP simulation.

Atlas model limitations

Unofficial fan project; not affiliated with The Isle, its publisher or ORIGIN. Atlas Score, solo/threat ratings, favored/danger lists, attribute bars, build recommendations, maturity and matchup ratios are authored estimates, not official balance values, live telemetry or win probabilities. The original September 2026 masses, role labels, staging suggestions, scores and qualitative matchups were not independently verified; unsupported advice is not displayed. Equal skill, healthy animals and neutral terrain are baseline assumptions. Terrain, player skill, attacks, hitboxes, stamina, bleed, positioning, and game updates can affect outcomes.

The normal model uses three slots and Prime four from 75% growth; the 25/50/75/75% acquisition stages are approximate. Build-fit uses a bounded top-14 search per slot and is capped by filled slots, not an exhaustive optimum. Conditional mutations and context scores are model inputs; bleeding and low health are triggers, not general injury penalties. Entomb uses historical/community Life 1/2/3+ tiers and does not simulate egg inheritance or actual server storage. Unquantified tiers retain their base model contribution; old picks stay in saved data but ineligible picks are excluded. Normal death and unofficial storage/restore may affect actual lineage behavior.

Public 0.22.157 fixes Xerocole Adaptation and Hematophagy values without publishing numbers. Existing tier values and score inputs remain historical. NHJ is an AI bug fix, not evidence of a selectable mutation. Per-species staging advice was removed because it had no supporting evidence.

Local storage keeps builds in the current browser; share URLs contain selected configurations and can be read by anyone with the link. This static app has no backend, account connection, tracking or server-side user database.

Calculation walkthrough

The software combines sourced mutation *effect descriptions* with site-authored assumptions. A cited 5% Hemomania effect does not validate the overall 1v1 ratio or chance of victory. The older growth ladder is a separate heuristic with different multipliers and thresholds.
